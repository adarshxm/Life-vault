## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a balance between high-security institutional trust and consumer-grade clarity. Designed for managing critical life assets, legal paperwork, warranties, and sensitive digital credentials, the visual narrative avoids sterile corporate enterprise motifs in favor of a crisp, modern architectural aesthetic.

Key principles:
- **Quiet Authority:** Premium institutional confidence delivered through pristine whites, deep slates, and measured negative space rather than heavy ornamentation.
- **Instrument Precision:** Financial and legal metadata presented with high tabular legibility, micro-badges, and structured metadata rows that respect user cognitive load.
- **Dynamic Vigilance:** Color serves as a functional signal—emerald for verified/secured assets, warm amber for approaching milestones, and rose for critical lapses or immediate action.
- **Tactile Soft-Elevation:** Subtle, layered depth that makes cards feel tangible, organized, and structurally anchored without visual clutter.

## Colors

The palette relies on a surgical light canvas paired with intentional semantic accents. 

- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - App Canvas / Ground: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50)
  - Card & Modal Surfaces: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
  - Subtle Surface Containers (Tints/Inlays): `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100)
  - Outlines & Dividers: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200)

- **Typography & Text Hierarchy:**
  - Primary Headers & Key Values: `#0F172A` (Slate 900)
  - Secondary Body & Labels: `#475569` (Slate 600)
  - Muted Metadata & Placeholder Text: `#94A3B8` (Slate 400)

- **Semantic & Action Tokens:**
  - Primary Interactive / Vault Blue: `#2563EB` (Electric Blue) for dominant calls-to-action, active toggles, and primary navigation focus.
  - Active / Healthy Status: `#10B981` (Emerald Green) paired with a 10% opacity wash (`#ECFDF5`) for active warranties and validated policies.
  - Expiring / Notice Status: `#F59E0B` (Warm Amber) paired with a 10% opacity wash (`#FFFBEB`) for renewals due within 30 days.
  - Critical / Urgent Status: `#EF4444` (Rose Red) paired with a 10% opacity wash (`#FEF2F2`) for expired coverage, lapses, and immediate authentication requirements.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans provides high clarity across complex numerical layouts, item registries, and administrative tables.

- **Numeric & Tabular Data:** All financial amounts, policy identification numbers, and dates must enforce `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1` to ensure tabular alignment in columns and ledger rows.
- **Weight Mapping:**
  - Bold (`700`) is restricted to primary section titles, large balances, and critical metric summaries.
  - Semi-Bold (`600`) governs component titles, list identifiers, and interactive elements.
  - Regular (`400`) handles body copy, item descriptions, and supportive helper notes.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Micro-labels (`label-xs`) are uppercase with wide tracking for status tags, category dividers, and asset classifications.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 8-point spatial model governs structural rhythm, supplemented by 4px increments for micro-alignments and high-density chips.

- **Grid Architecture:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column responsive fluid grid with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Maximum container constraint capped at `1280px` for optimal eye-tracking across asset lists.
  - **Tablet (640px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `margin: 1.5rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`.
  - **Mobile (<640px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin-mobile: 1rem` and `gutter-mobile: 1rem`. Cards stretch edge-to-edge within the margin boundaries.
- **Component Density:**
  - Card internal padding defaults to `space-lg` (1.5rem) on desktop, shifting down to `space-md` (1rem) on mobile screens.
  - Dense metadata rows and table list items utilize `space-sm` (0.5rem) vertical padding with `space-md` (1rem) horizontal padding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses crisp boundary delineation and soft ambient layering, reflecting the structural security of a personal vault.

- **Layering Principle:**
  - Canvas (`#F8FAFC`) acts as the foundation base.
  - Interactive cards, summary vaults, and list items sit elevated on pure `#FFFFFF`.
  - Nested components (e.g., policy breakdown inside an asset card, input text boxes) sit inside `#F1F5F9` surfaces.
- **Borders & Dividers:**
  - Every card and elevated module must be bound by a 1px border of `#E2E8F0` to maintain definition regardless of display calibration.
- **Shadow System:**
  - *Resting Card:* `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
  - *Interactive Hover:* `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
  - *Modals & Flying Vault Trays:* `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The interface utilizes a rounded geometry (`roundedness: 2`) that introduces welcoming curves without losing structured utility.

- **Scale Configuration:**
  - Base components (inputs, list rows, small buttons): `0.5rem` (8px).
  - Main cards, modular tiles, and panels: `rounded-lg` at `1rem` (16px).
  - Sheet dialogs, critical onboarding modals, and vault containers: `rounded-xl` at `1.5rem` (24px).
  - Status indicators, tag chips, and icon containers: Pill-shaped (`9999px`) to distinguish categorical tags from interactive container cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#2563EB` with `#FFFFFF` text. Height `40px` (desktop) / `48px` (mobile). Hover state deepens to `#1D4ED8`. Active state uses `scale(0.98)` transition.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Pure white surface with `#E2E8F0` border and `#0F172A` text. Hover uses `#F8FAFC` background.
- **Destructive:** Bordered or tinted red (`#FEF2F2` background, `#DC2626` text).

### Status Chips & Badges
- High-density pills (`height: 24px`, font size: `label-xs`, uppercase, bold).
- **Active / Valid:** `#ECFDF5` background, `#047857` text, with a 6px solid `#10B981` dot on the leading edge.
- **Expiring Soon:** `#FFFBEB` background, `#B45309` text, with a 6px solid `#F59E0B` dot on the leading edge.
- **Lapsed / Critical:** `#FEF2F2` background, `#B91C1C` text, with a 6px solid `#EF4444` dot on the leading edge.
- **Category Tags:** `#F1F5F9` background, `#475569` text, rounded `0.375rem`.

### Asset & Document Cards
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`. Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Padding: `1.5rem`.
- Layout: Top row houses the asset icon, category badge, and menu trigger. Middle section displays primary asset title (`title-md`) and estimated value/coverage (`headline-sm` with tabular numbers). Bottom row features status chip and expiration date.
- Hover state: Elevation lifts smoothly (`transition: all 0.2s cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`), subtle border tint transition toward `#CBD5E1`.

### Data Lists & Ledger Tables
- Flat, alternating or border-separated rows. Border bottom: `1px solid #F1F5F9`.
- Column alignments: Left-align descriptions and document titles; right-align monetary sums, policy dates, and tabular numbers.
- Hover: Entire row highlights with `#F8FAFC`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: `1px solid #CBD5E1`. Padding: `0.625rem 0.875rem`.
- Text: `#0F172A`, placeholder: `#94A3B8`.
- Focus: Border switches to `#2563EB` with a `0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)` focus ring.
- Error state: Border `#EF4444` with `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.15)` focus ring.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: `18px x 18px`. Border: `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`.
- Checked state: Filled `#2563EB` with pure white checkmark icon. Smooth spring scaling transition on toggle.
